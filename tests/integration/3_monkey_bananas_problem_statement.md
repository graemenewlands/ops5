The problems to be solved in this section (section 3) is a version of the "Monkey and Bananas Problem", which is described as follows:

In a 10' x 10' x 10' room, there is:
- a heavy couch on the floor
- a light ladder on the floor
- a bunch of bananas (eitehr suspended from the ceiling or on the couch, ladder or floor)
- one very, very hungry monkey who is incapable of moving heavy objects and
- the monkey's blanket

write a program that will read in a description of the objects and their locations in the room and produce as output a sequence of commands that give the monkey that if followed, will allow the monkey to grab the bunch of bananas.
